**Keyfinder** turns the Group Finder into a Mythic+ tool. It ranks every listing for *your* character, filters down to the keys that actually upgrade your runs, and gives group leaders a clear read on their applicants.

### Find a group

- **Match score** for every listing, based on your role, your timed best in that dungeon, your rating compared with the leader's, the Bloodlust or battle res you bring, friends in the group and auto-accept.
- **Dungeon strip** with your timed best on each dungeon and how many open groups each one has. Click to filter, right-click for that dungeon only.
- **Group details**: dungeon art, group title, accept and timing chances, the composition with Bloodlust and battle res icons, the leader's best in that dungeon, and the reasons behind the score.
- **Upgrades mode**: type a level (for example `19`) in the Group Finder search. At your base level Keyfinder shows only the dungeons where that key is an upgrade for you; a level above it shows every dungeon. Base level and range are configurable.
- Native-style **Filters** menu: leader rating, leader's best, listing age, playstyle, role slots, rating requirement, Bloodlust, battle res, auto-accept.
- Your active applications sit in the footer and at the top of the list.

### Your season

- One card per dungeon with your best, your points and the next step, weakest dungeons first.
- The level you still need, shown right at the top.

### Lead a group

- Your listing, your key and your current composition.
- **Recommendations**: missing roles, missing Bloodlust or battle res (and which classes bring them), what best run to look for at your key level.
- **Applicant insights**: how many are waiting per role, average rating, how many have already timed your key.
- Applicants sorted by fit, the best one for each open role marked as recommended, with Invite and Decline right on the row.
- **Parse Academy** button on every applicant and on group leaders. It uses the Parse Academy addon when installed, or shows a link to copy.

### Alerts

- Rules like "this dungeon, leader 3000+, best +16, spot for my role".
- Checked on every search, even with the window closed: sound, taskbar flash, on-screen warning and a popup with an Apply button.
- One click to bind searching to F8.

### Opening Keyfinder

- `/kf`, the Keyfinder tab on the side of the Group Finder, the button in the Group Finder title bar, the minimap addon compartment, or a keybind.

### Honest by design

- The game hides group titles from addons, so Keyfinder **never guesses the key level** on screen. The level you see is the group's own title, or the number you searched for.
- Searching, applying, inviting and declining always need your click, as the game requires. There's no auto-apply.
- Keyfinder only adds its own frames and never modifies Blizzard's, so it won't taint your UI.
