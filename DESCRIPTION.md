Keyfinder helps you find Mythic+ groups worth joining without scrolling through the whole Group Finder.

It looks at every listing and puts the groups that suit your character at the top. For each one you can see the open roles, whether they have Bloodlust and a battle res, how far the leader has pushed that dungeon, and what you would bring. When you're chasing a specific level, search for it (say, 19) and Keyfinder hides the dungeons where you already have that key timed, so you only see the runs that actually move you forward.

There's also a simple overview of your season: your best run in each dungeon, the ones that need work, and how many groups are open for each of them right now.

If you're the one forming the group, Keyfinder shows what your group is still missing and which classes would fill the gap. Applicants are sorted by how well they fit your key, the strongest pick for each role is highlighted, and you can invite, decline or look someone up on Parse Academy straight from the list.

You can also set alerts, like "Ara-Kara with a healer spot and a 3000+ leader", and Keyfinder will let you know as soon as one shows up, even with the window closed.

Open it with /kf or from the Keyfinder button on the Group Finder. Available in English and Brazilian Portuguese.
